**Plan: Free.** Uses 1 of 1 iCal feed and no connections.

Someone needs to book around you. They do not need to know that Thursday afternoon is a hospital appointment.

## What you build

One [iCal feed](/docs/ical-feeds) on its default settings — times only, every event titled "Busy" — handed to the person who needs it.

## Steps

1. Open **iCal Feeds** and open your feed.
2. Under **Calendars**, tick only the calendars whose time should count as busy. A calendar of tentative someday plans probably should not be in there.
3. Leave Feed Settings alone. The defaults are the private ones: names, descriptions and locations all off.
4. Check the disclosure line under the link. It should read that the link shares event times only. That line is generated from the live settings, so it is the honest answer rather than a promise.
5. Send the link.

## Treat the link like a password

A feed link carries no sign-in: whoever holds it can subscribe. That is what makes it easy to share and what makes it worth being careful with. If a link spreads further than you meant, delete the feed — subscribers stop receiving events — and build a new one.

## When one link is not enough

Different people warrant different amounts of detail: your assistant may need titles, a client does not. That needs a second feed, which needs Pro, since Free includes one. On Pro, make one feed per audience and give each the settings that audience should have.
