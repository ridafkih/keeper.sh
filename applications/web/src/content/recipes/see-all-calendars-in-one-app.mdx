Subscribe to one Keeper.sh link in the app you actually check, and every calendar you have connected appears there. Nothing is copied and nothing is written into any calendar — your week is simply visible in one place.

## What you build

One calendar link you can subscribe to — a [shareable calendar link](/docs/shareable-calendar-links) — carrying every calendar you have connected.

## Steps

1. Connect the accounts whose calendars you want to see. Free allows two.
2. Open **iCal Feeds** in the dashboard and open your feed.
3. Under **Calendars**, tick every calendar that should appear.
4. Read the line under the feed link. On Free it says the link shares event times only, and every event reads "Busy".
5. Copy the link and subscribe in your calendar app — usually **Add calendar**, then **From URL** or **Subscribe**.

## Why this suits the Free plan

Connections are the scarce thing on Free — you get three — and this link costs none of them. A calendar that only needs to be *seen* somewhere should travel this way. [Adding another calendar is blocked by a plan limit](/guides/blocked-by-a-plan-limit) makes the same point from the other side.

## What it will not do

- **It is read-only.** You cannot create events in a subscribed link — keep creating them in the real calendars.
- **It refreshes on the app's schedule.** Your calendar app checks the link when it chooses, and some apps leave hours between checks. If a stale view is the problem, read [a subscribed calendar is showing out-of-date events](/guides/subscribed-calendar-out-of-date).
- **On Free it shows times only.** Getting your own titles back in your own feed is a Pro feed setting.
