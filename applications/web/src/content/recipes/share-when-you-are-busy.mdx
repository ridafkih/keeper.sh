Hand someone a link that shows when you are busy and nothing else. They see blocks of time labelled "Busy" — no titles, no notes, no locations — so they can book around you without knowing that Thursday afternoon is a hospital appointment.

## What you build

One calendar link the other person can subscribe to — a [shareable calendar link](/docs/shareable-calendar-links) — on its default settings.

## Steps

1. Open **iCal Feeds** in the dashboard and open your feed.
2. Under **Calendars**, tick only the calendars whose time should count as busy. Leave tentative someday plans out.
3. Leave **Feed Settings** alone. The defaults are the private ones: names, descriptions and locations all off.
4. Check the line under the link says it shares event times only. That line comes from the live settings, so it reflects what the link actually gives away.
5. Send the link.

## Treat the link like a password

The link needs no sign-in: anyone who holds it can subscribe. That makes it easy to share, and worth guarding. If it spreads further than you meant, delete the feed — everyone subscribed stops receiving events — and create a new one.

## When one link is not enough

Different people deserve different amounts of detail: an assistant may need titles, a client does not. That takes a second feed, which needs Pro — Free includes one. On Pro, make one feed per audience with the settings that audience should have.
