**Plan: Pro.** Uses 2 linked accounts, 2 connections, and the per-calendar sync settings.

You want your personal commitments to hold their slot on your work calendar, without a colleague — or an administrator with access to it — reading what they are.

## What you build

- Work calendar → personal calendar, with full detail
- Personal calendar → work calendar, with the title, description and location stripped

Same two connections as [work and personal calendar in both directions](/recipes/work-and-personal-both-ways); the difference is entirely in the settings on one of them.

## Steps

1. Set up both connections as in that recipe.
2. Open the **personal** calendar — the one events are read *from* in the private direction — and find **Sync Settings**.
3. Turn **Sync Event Name** off. The **Event Name** field becomes editable and starts at `{{calendar_name}}`; replace it with whatever you want the block to say. "Busy" and "Personal" are the usual answers.
4. Turn **Sync Event Description** and **Sync Event Location** off.
5. Leave the **work** calendar's settings alone, so copies going the other way keep their titles, notes and joining links.

## Why the settings live where they do

Sync settings sit on the calendar events are read *from*, and they apply to everything that calendar sends. That is what makes this recipe work: the personal calendar is the source in the private direction and the destination in the detailed one, so stripping detail there affects only the events leaving it. [Sync settings](/docs/sync-settings) covers the whole section.

It also means the setting is not per destination. If your personal calendar sends to two places and only one should be discreet, this shape cannot express that — use a [feed](/docs/ical-feeds) for the discreet audience instead.

## Tuning it

- **Exclusions** can keep whole categories out of work entirely — all-day birthdays being the obvious one.
- **Sync Window** on the work calendar keeps the far future out of it, though narrowing a range also clears out copies already beyond it: [sync window](/docs/sync-window).
- If the copies come out blank when you wanted them named, you have the direction inverted — [why your copied events say Busy](/guides/copied-events-say-busy).
