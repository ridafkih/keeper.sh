Three counts and one interval separate the plans. Everything else — the providers you can connect, the way copies are kept corrected, the privacy defaults — is the same on both.

## The counts

| | Free | Pro |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Linked accounts | 2 | Unlimited |
| Connections | 3 | Unlimited |
| iCal feeds | 1 | Unlimited |

A linked account is one connected provider, however many calendars sit inside it. A connection is one source calendar sending to one destination calendar. A feed is one subscribable link. The [accounts](/docs/accounts-and-calendars), [connections](/docs/connections) and [feeds](/docs/ical-feeds) pages each cover what the thing being counted actually is.

When you are at a limit, the dashboard says so where the action would have been rather than failing after the fact.

## How quickly changes land

Your calendars are checked every minute on both plans. On Free a change reaches your other calendars within 30 minutes; on Pro, within a minute.

## What Pro unlocks

- The [sync window](/docs/sync-window) on a calendar receiving events
- [Sync settings](/docs/sync-settings) — the event name template, and the description and location toggles
- Exclusions on a connection, and event filters on a feed
- Feed settings, so a feed can carry more than times

## Price

Pro is $5 a month, or $42 a year paid annually. Billing runs through Polar, and the customer portal linked from your account settings handles changes and cancellation. The [pricing page](/pricing) carries the current figures and the plan comparison.

## Self-hosting

Keeper.sh is open source under AGPL-3.0. A self-hosted instance runs outside commercial mode, so every account on it gets the Pro feature set with no plan limits at all. [Open-source calendar sync](/blog/open-source-calendar-sync) covers the wider landscape if you are weighing that up.
