You created an event, switched to the other calendar, and it is not there. Start at the top of this list — the first two causes account for most of it.

## Has enough time passed?

Your calendars are checked every minute on both plans, but delivery is not instant. A change reaches your other calendars within 30 minutes on Free and within a minute on Pro. If you are on Free and it has been two minutes, that is normal; wait out the half hour before treating it as a fault.

Calendar apps add their own delay on top. A subscribed link is refreshed on the app's schedule, not yours — see [why the calendar you subscribed to is out of date](/guides/subscribed-link-out-of-date).

## Does the connection point the way you think?

Connections go one way. A connection from A to B copies events created in A into B, and does nothing at all for events created in B.

Open the calendar the event was created in and check **Send Events to Calendars**. If the destination is not ticked there, no connection exists in that direction, however complete the setup looks from the other side. [Connections](/docs/connections) covers setting up the second direction.

## Is the event inside the sync window?

The calendar receiving events has a sync window: one month back and two years ahead by default. An event outside it is not copied, which is what usually explains a missing event far in the future or one dragged well into the past. On Pro the ranges can be widened under **Sync Window**, up to two years in each direction. [Sync window](/docs/sync-window) has the detail.

## Is the event being excluded?

Exclusions leave whole categories out: all-day events, and on Google, focus time and out-of-office blocks. If the thing that will not copy is a birthday, a holiday or a focus block, check **Exclusions** on the source calendar.

## Are you at a limit?

If you set the connection up recently and it did not take, you may have hit the connection limit — 3 on Free. [Why you cannot connect another calendar](/guides/cannot-connect-another-calendar) covers what that looks like.

## Is the account still connected?

A provider can revoke access: a password change, a revoked app-specific password, an administrator policy. If an account has stopped working, reconnect it from the dashboard and the queued changes flow again.
