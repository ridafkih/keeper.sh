An option is greyed out, or the dashboard says you have reached a limit. Keeper.sh Free allows 2 linked accounts, 3 connections and 1 shareable link — and Pro removes all three limits. Which of the three you ran out of decides what to do next.

## Which limit did you hit?

- **Linked accounts** — each Google, Outlook, iCloud or other calendar login you connect. Free allows 2.
- **Connections** — each one copies events from one calendar into another. Free allows 3.
- **Shareable links** — read-only calendar links other apps and people can subscribe to. Free allows 1.

[Plans and limits](/docs/plans-and-limits) sets all three out in a table.

## One login counts as one account

Connecting a Google account brings in every calendar inside it, and that is one linked account. Two Google logins are two accounts, even if you only want one calendar from each.

Before removing anything, check whether the calendar you want is already inside an account you linked earlier. It often is.

## Sending events both ways uses two connections

Sending one calendar into two others is two connections. A pair of calendars copying to each other is also two. One pair like that plus a single extra direction fills the Free plan exactly.

If you are over, ask whether every direction earns its place. Seeing your work day in your personal calendar takes one connection. Copying the other way as well only matters if you create work events in the personal calendar.

## One shareable link frees up several connections

A calendar that only needs to be *seen* — on a phone, a shared screen, a second app — needs no connection at all. One shareable link can carry every calendar you have, and it writes nothing into anyone's calendar. The [see all your calendars in one app](/recipes/see-all-calendars-in-one-app) recipe builds exactly that.

## Already on Pro?

Pro has no limits, so a refusal there is something else. Either the account lost access at Google or Outlook, or the calendar cannot receive events.

A calendar you subscribed to by link is read-only and can never receive copies. [Linked accounts and calendars](/docs/accounts-and-calendars) covers which calendars can do what.
